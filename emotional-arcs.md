# Emotional Pacing Reference

> **Reference layer.** Read by the orchestrator and reviewers. Never read by the build subagent — compile applicable pacing into the brief.

These are possible reader experiences, not emotional requirements. Use only supported events and the actual audience starting position. No invented reversal, false trail, opponent, victory, anecdote or mandatory quotable line. Preserve all uncertainty, including at reveals and scenario forks. A direct, low-intensity treatment is valid.

| Arc | Possible experience | Pacing option when useful |
|---|---|---|
| Prestige | Familiar expectation → supported contradiction → reinterpretation | Give the contradiction space, then give the full replacement explanation and end on it; do not hide caveats. |
| Mystery Box | Question → accumulating clues → explanation | Supply context needed to reason. A documented failed hypothesis can be explored; never invent a red herring. Busy readers may stop before the reveal. |
| Heist | Goal → obstacles → capabilities → execution | Allocate time to the real difficulty and plan. No promised triumph without support. |
| Time Machine | Possible futures → causes → present choice | Clearly label assumptions and keep conditional language throughout. |
| Trojan Horse | Familiar case → deeper implication → new frame | Explain why the case warrants the broader implication. Do not manipulate agreement with false premises. |
| Hero's Journey | Starting position → real challenges → supported transformation | Let the importance of events determine their space, without demanding danger or catharsis. For evaluators, the protagonist is not the author. |
| Freytag | Developing conflict → turning point → consequences | Build only the tension in the source. Resolution can remain incomplete. |
| Columbo | Known outcome → reconstruction → causal understanding | Reveal the outcome early, then give the mechanism sufficient space. |
| Game of the Scene | Observation → recurring examples → pattern | Use as many supported examples as needed, without a fixed escalation count or compulsory aphorism. |
| Rashomon | Different accounts → comparison → synthesis or bounded disagreement | Fair treatment is evidence-weighted, not mandatory equal time or a claim all views are equally true. End by saying which claims survive. |

## Audience calibration

Ask or infer desired intensity from the actual occasion and preferences, not job title. Explain any inferred choice in the brief. Humor, intimacy and urgency are optional. A formal tone can still be engaging through relevance and specificity. A playful tone does not relax source fidelity.

## Review

Evaluate whether pacing makes the reasoning easier to follow and the payoff relevant. Do not fail a plain piece for lacking a prescribed emotion, dramatic peak or rhythm. For answer-first work, the reason to continue can be understanding the evidence behind an already-stated answer.
