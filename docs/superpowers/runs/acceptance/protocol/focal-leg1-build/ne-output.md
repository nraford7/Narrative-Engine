# Q3 retention review for the budget offsite

## Retention metrics held steady in Q3, with GRR down 1.1 percentage points.

Gross revenue retention was 91.2%, down 1.1 percentage points quarter on quarter. Annualized logo churn was 4.8%; NPS was 41. Renewal cohort performance was within range across segments: enterprise GRR was 94.6%, mid-market 89.9%, SMB 86.1%.

Competitive pressure on price continues. Renewal decisions took nine days longer on average, consistent with broader procurement tightening.

## CS completed its reorganization with headcount flat.

The CS reorganization completed in August, and the new health-score dashboard is live for enterprise accounts. Average ticket response is 4.1 hours. Headcount is flat.

## Choose option (c) at the offsite to reallocate AED 1.8M to guided onboarding.

Sales has requested an expanded Q4 retention-discount pool; the current pool is AED 2.6M per year. The offsite options include expanding the pool, holding it flat, or reallocating AED 1.8M to guided onboarding.

Choose the reallocation to fund a guided-onboarding service targeting a first saved report inside 14 days for all new mid-market and SMB accounts. This choice requires sales to absorb tougher price conversations in Q4.
