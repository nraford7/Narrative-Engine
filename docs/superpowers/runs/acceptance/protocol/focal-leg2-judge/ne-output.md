# Q3 retention review for the Q4 budget offsite

## Q3 retention held steady within segment ranges, with GRR down 1.1 percentage points.

Gross revenue retention was 91.2%, down 1.1 percentage points quarter on quarter. Annualized logo churn was 4.8%; NPS was 41. Renewal cohort performance was within range across segments: enterprise GRR was 94.6%, mid-market 89.9%, SMB 86.1%.

Competitive pressure on price continues. Three of the seven largest churned accounts cited cost in exit interviews. Renewal decisions took nine days longer on average, consistent with broader procurement tightening.

## CS completed its reorganization, and headcount remains flat.

The CS reorganization completed in August. Average ticket response is 4.1 hours, and the new health-score dashboard is live for enterprise accounts.

## Choose option (c) at the offsite: reallocate AED 1.8M to guided onboarding.

Sales has requested an expanded Q4 retention-discount pool; the current pool is AED 2.6M per year. The offsite options are to approve that expansion, hold the pool flat, or reallocate AED 1.8M of the pool to guided onboarding.

Choose the reallocation. The service would target a first saved report within 14 days for all new mid-market and SMB accounts. This choice requires sales to absorb tougher price conversations in Q4.
