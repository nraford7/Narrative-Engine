# Voice Profiles

> **Reference layer.** Read by the orchestrator and reviewers, never directly by the writer. Compile only relevant instructions into the brief.

Voice is a suggested treatment, not a personality assigned to an audience. User preferences, purpose, evidence, the approved outline and practical limits outrank every profile. No quota for rhetorical questions, anecdotes, numbers, humor, sentence lengths or emotional turns. Preserve every necessary qualification; never make a recommendation sound like an established fact.

## Seven available voices

| Voice | Possible treatment | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Boardroom | Direct sentences, clear implications, economical explanations. | No obligatory urgency, numeric anchors or action request. Uncertainty remains visible. |
| TED Stage | Accessible explanation, varied spoken rhythm, concrete examples. | No invented personal anecdote, required revelation or rehearsed inspiration. |
| Provocateur | State a supported challenge to an assumption clearly. | Do not manufacture controversy, caricature alternatives or overstate evidence. |
| Investigator | Concrete observations, careful reasoning and transparent limits. | The approved outline decides whether the answer comes first. No invented mystery or withheld essential context. |
| Storyteller | Develop supported events, people and consequences with readable pacing. | No fabricated scenes, composite testimony or dialogue presented as fact. Plain exposition is valid. |
| Academic | Precise terms, evidence, methods and qualifications where useful. | No compulsory long sentences, uniform paragraphs or prohibition on a supported recommendation. |
| Dry Wit | Occasional understatement or humor when appropriate. | No joke quota and no distortion of a claim for a punchline. |

## Compile and confirm

Translate the selected voice into a few useful instructions for this assignment. Example: "Use clear sentences, explain sample limitations, and avoid campaign language." Accept descriptions such as "a skeptical journalist" without requiring a profile name. A blend uses a primary voice plus only compatible secondary guidance. Voice cannot change the evidence selected as true or the approved reasoning order.
